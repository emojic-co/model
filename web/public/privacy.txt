Privacy Policy — emojify.ing
Last updated: September 16, 2026.

emojify.ing (web app at https://emojify.ing/ and the Android app, package
ing.emojify) turns short text into an emoji, a feeling label, and a color
palette using a small machine-learning model.

What we collect
----------------
Nothing. The model runs entirely on your device (in your browser via
WebAssembly, or on-device in the Android app via ONNX Runtime). The text you
type is never sent to a server — there is no backend for this app to send it
to. We don't use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs, and the
Android app requests no INTERNET permission.

Local storage
-------------
Your browser may cache the app's static files (including the model) for
faster loads, and the Android app may save a rendered card image to your
device only when you explicitly choose to share or copy it. None of this
leaves your device.

Third-party embeds
-------------------
The web app embeds a GitHub "star" button (served from ghbtns.com) that
shows the project's star count. Loading it fetches from GitHub's servers the
same way any embedded image would; we don't control what GitHub does with
that request.

Children
--------
This app isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect any
information from anyone, regardless of age — because it doesn't collect
information at all.
