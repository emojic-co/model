Our emoji annotations have 2 characteristics:
1. Each text is associated with just a few emojis out of ~1,000 possible emojis.
2. We assumes there are many false negatives, i.e. for each text, there are more emojis that could be associated with it, but were not annotated.

Under these assumptions, write a document doc/loss_function.md that describe our dataset and several loss functions that are appropriate for these settings.
