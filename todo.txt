Selecting Keywords and Training Strategy:
    Select all keywords from data/ii.json that:
        - are 3 - 6 chars long inclusive
        - have at least one emoji in our dataset

    1. Run a trained EmojiHead on these keywords
    2. Order by rank descending. (rank is the first place the model predicts emoji from the keyword emoji set)
    3. use the top 1000 keywords as the tf-vector

    Training:
        Start the backprop from the very end of the FusionHead, not from both the EmojiHead and KWHead.
        The goal is to split the learning between the two heads and not to force each head to learn the entire space.
