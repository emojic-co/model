I want to avoid as much as we can using the energy loss directly to train the generator.
The reason is that while it lower the energy distance the final results are more often than not do not match the text.
I tried to use just the `color_critic` and just the `cond_color_critic` but, while lowering the energy distance a bit, they do not lower it enough.
The final results also look bad.
I think we must change something else than just balance between the loss functions.
Either change the models, the training or both.
I prefer to modify the models over changing the train procedure.

We might want to try (again) to concat the noise to the text in the generator.
Please, if you choose to implement it, use the (already defined) `Z_WEIGHT` to control the dimension of the noise.
The dimension of the noise should be `Z_WEIGHT * TEXT_EMBEDDING_SIZE` and the noise should be concatenated to the text embedding before being passed to the generator.

If you have any other suggestions, please let me know.
