        New data pipeline, split out of gen_data.ts into three scripts:
            bun raw_txt.ts -> bun annotation.ts -> bun gen_labels.ts
        (package.json: bun run raw / annotate / labels)

        Step 1: Generate raw text (raw_txt.ts):
            - diverse, informal, short (<= 50 chars) WhatsApp-style messages
            - appends to raw.txt (never truncates)
            - deduplicated against itself and existing raw.txt via data.py's normalize key
        Step 2: Label each text with emoji and feeling (annotation.ts):
            - reads raw.txt, skips lines longer than MAX_TEXT_LEN and lines already in data.jsonl
            - annotates in batches: emoji free choice, feeling from labels.json feelings
            - appends records to data.jsonl, removes handled lines from raw.txt (retries the rest)
        Step 3: (re)generate labels.json (gen_labels.ts):
            - feelings copied through untouched (fixed closed set)
            - emojis in data.jsonl tallied, sorted by frequency desc (ties keep first-seen order)
            - top 100 written back to labels.json in that order
        Step 4: runtime filtering in data.py read():
            - drops records whose feeling or emoji is not in labels.json
            - drops records whose normalized text is longer than MAX_TEXT_LEN
            - data.jsonl is never modified; filtering is runtime-only
