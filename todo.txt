Upsample Colors:
    We start with the following list:
        red, green, blue, dark, bright

    Steps:
        1. Ask the text generator to generate text that are related to the colors, text can contain the given color or implicitly related to the color.
        2. Send the text to the annotator and accept the labels.
        3. Add {color: <color>} to the records.

    Run with bun upsample --colors --per 1000
