upsample:
    add an option
    upsample --lang=he
    top upsample records in Hebrew text.
    final record should be of the form {text: '', emojis:'', styles:[], bg:[], fg:'', lang:'he'}
    if the lang field is missing it means the record is in English.
    lang field should never stripped from the record by any tool including the `tools/data/minimize.ts` one.

    Note 
        When running `upsample` without any argument it just generate text and annotate them.
        it generates --count=100 records by default.
