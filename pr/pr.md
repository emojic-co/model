# pr/

Marketing and launch material for **emojify.ing** (https://emojify.ing).

## Layout

- `common/` — shared resources every audience draws from: product fact sheet,
  positioning notes, boilerplate blurbs, screenshot/GIF shot-list, links.
- One subfolder per target audience, holding copy tuned to that audience and
  its channels.
- `remotion/` — a Remotion template that films the real emojify.ing UI doing a
  typewriter demo (phrase types itself, the card transforms live). Produces
  1080×1920 clips for social. See `remotion/README.md`.

## Audiences

- `casual/` — general / social sharers (Reddit, X, TikTok/Reels). Playful,
  no jargon: "type anything, get a vibe card." _(in progress)_
- `teens/` — teens & kids. Short, punchy, meme-aware. See `teens/taglines.md`.

Planned later: `hn-devs/`, `product-hunt/`, `designers/`.

## Status

Early. Building one deliverable at a time and iterating.
