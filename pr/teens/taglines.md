# Taglines — teens & kids

Short, punchy, meme-aware copy for **emojify.ing** aimed at a teen/kid
audience. The product: type a short phrase → animated vibe card with emojis,
colors (gradient background + text color), and a font/motion style.

## Play on the name (emojify + .ing)

- emojify anything.
- you type it, we emojify.ing
- stop texting. start emojify.ing
- currently: emojify.ing
- the verb for your vibe
- make it a whole mood, -ing

## Vibe-check energy

- type a thought. get a whole vibe.
- your words, but make it a mood
- every sentence has a color. we found it.
- turn "idk how i feel" into a card
- feelings, but make them shareable
- it reads the room in your text

## Short + punchy (bio / sticker length)

- words in, vibe out
- caption → card
- say less, feel more
- from typing to *feeling* in one tap
- a mood generator for your group chat
- screenshot-ready feelings

## Group-chat / share angle

- the flex your captions were missing
- make your story slide actually hit
- drop it in the chat. watch it transform.
- your text just got a glow-up

## Top 5 picks

1. words in, vibe out — clean, meme-able, works as a sticker
2. you type it, we emojify.ing — leans on the domain, sounds like a verb
3. type a thought. get a whole vibe. — says exactly what it does
4. your text just got a glow-up — familiar slang, shows transformation
5. a mood generator for your group chat — names the use case directly

## Note

Slang like "glow-up" / "hits different" dates fast and can read try-hard to
older teens. The name-based and plainspoken lines ("words in, vibe out",
"type a thought, get a whole vibe") will age better.
