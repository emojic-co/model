Goals for this iteration — per-leaf detail 2026-09-12-52017be.yml

Priority 1 (keyword) and priority 2 (term) are both maxed out (0.99-1.0 vs 0.90/0.80 hard targets) after the fusion-combiner removal and vocab growth 287->400->711 across the untracked runs since the last plan. Priority 3 (full-text emoji Acc) is now the focus: acc@1 amber (0.615 vs 0.75 hard, clears the old 0.56 iter target), acc@5/@10 red but within 0.005-0.015 of their hard targets (0.795/0.835 vs 0.80/0.85). TensorBoard shows MRR/e/val plateaued and noisy (0.758-0.773) while MRR/e/train keeps climbing to 0.79 - a growing train/val gap, not an undertrained model. The report's own block_capacity section explains the mechanism: the deepest, most-dilated encoder block (dilation 8) contributes 40.1% of the pooled embedding on keyword rows but only 12.9% on full-text eval rows, and RECEPTIVE_FIELD (31 chars, from `uv run python model/config.py`) doesn't cover MAX_TEXT_LEN (42) - a structural/architecture-shaped gap, not a data or LR problem. Targets below hold at current for every priority-4-and-below goal since priority 3 is still open (color energy, style, max text len, vocab size are all below focus despite 4/6 color-energy legs being red).
